# Cartboost - Go-To-Market Strategy

## Product Overview

**Cartboost** is an A/B testing app for Shopify merchants that helps optimize conversion rates through controlled experiments.

---

## Target Market

### Primary Audience
- Shopify merchants (5M+ stores)
- E-commerce businesses
- Conversion-focused store owners
- Growth hackers

### Ideal Customer
- **Revenue:** $10K-500K/year
- **Size:** Small to medium business
- **Focus:** Conversion optimization
- **Tech:** Comfortable with apps

---

## Value Proposition

### Problem
- Merchants guess which product pages convert
- No data-driven decisions
- Low conversion rates

### Solution
- A/B test any element
- Data-driven decisions
- Increase conversions

### Result
- Higher conversion rates
- More revenue
- Better UX

---
