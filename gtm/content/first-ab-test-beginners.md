## Section 1: What is A/B Testing? (Plain English)

### The Simple Definition

A/B testing (also called split testing) is showing two versions of something to different visitors and measuring which one gets more conversions.

That's it.

### A Simple Example

Imagine you have two headlines:

- **Version A:** "Premium Coffee Beans"
- **Version B:** "artisanal Coffee Beans, Roasted Fresh"

You show Version A to 500 visitors and Version B to 500 visitors.

**Results:**
- Version A: 2% conversion (10 sales)
- Version B: 4% conversion (20 sales)

Version B is performing **double** Version A. Now you know what works.

That's A/B testing. Let your customers decide what's best—not your gut.

### Why It Works

Different visitors respond to different things. A/B testing removes the guesswork by measuring actual behavior. When you test, you're making decisions based on data, not opinions.

## Section 2: What You Should Test First (Priority Order)

Not all tests are created equal. Some changes have huge potential impact; others barely move the needle.

Here's what to test first, ranked by potential impact:

## Section 4: How to Know When You're Done

### The Only Statistic You Need to Know

**Statistical significance** sounds complicated, but here's all you need to know:

> **Your result is trustworthy when you reach 95% statistical significance.**

When Cartboost shows "95% significance," it means there's only a 5% chance the result is random. That's good enough to act on.

### When to Stop Your Test

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Reached 95% significance** | ✅ Declare winner |
| **Ran for 2 weeks with no result** | ❌ Stop—no difference |
| **One version is winning significantly** | ✅ Stop early—declare winner |

### When to Keep Going

| Condition | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| **Below 95% significance** | Keep testing |
| **Not enough traffic** | Let it run longer |

**Pro Tip:** If your store gets less than 100 visitors per day, run each test for at least 2 weeks to get meaningful data.

## Section 5: 10 Quick Tests for Any Shopify Store

Here are 10 tests you can run right now—just swap in your products and store details:

### 1. Headline Test
**What:** Change your main headline
**Why:** First impression = biggest impact
**Example:** "Premium Coffee" vs "Fresh Roasted Coffee"

### 2. Hero Image Test
**What:** Product-only vs lifestyle image
**Why:** Emotional connection
**Example:** Bean photo vs person drinking coffee

### 3. CTA Color Test
**What:** Different button colors
**Why:** Colors trigger different actions
**Example:** Green vs Orange vs Red

### 4. CTA Text Test
**What:** Button copy variations
**Why:** Words drive action
**Example:** "Add to Cart" vs "Buy Now" vs "Get Yours"

### 5. Price Display Test
**What:** How you show price
**Why:** Framing matters
**Example:** "$100" vs "From $100" vs "$100 + Free Shipping"

### 6. Trust Badges Test
**What:** Add/remove security badges
**Why:** Reduces purchase anxiety
**Example:** Without badges vs "Secure Checkout" + "Money Back Guarantee"

### 7. Product Description Test
**What:** Short vs long description
**Why:** Depends on your product
**Example:** 3 bullets vs full paragraph + bullet combo

### 8. Reviews Visibility Test
**What:** Where reviews appear
**Why:** Trust signals early = more confidence
**Example:** Below fold vs above fold

### 9. Shipping Message Test
**What:** Free shipping threshold messaging
**Why:** Increases average order value
**Example:** No message vs "Free shipping over $50"

### 10. Urgency Message Test
**What:** Scarcity messaging
**Why:** Drives immediate action
**Example:** No message vs "Only 3 left in stock"
