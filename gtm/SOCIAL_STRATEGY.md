## Platform Strategies

### X/Twitter Strategy

**Why:** Best for SaaS, B2B, developers, Shopify merchants

**Target Audience:** 
- Shopify store owners
- Ecommerce merchants
- Marketing professionals
- SaaS founders

**Posting Schedule:**
- 2-3 posts/day
- Best times: 9 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM EST

**Content Mix:**
| Type | % | Example |
|------|---|---------|
| Tips | 30% | "A/B testing best practices" |
| Questions | 25% | "What will you test?" |
| Features | 20% | "New feature: heatmaps" |
| Case Studies | 15% | "Store X increased 34%" |
| Engagement | 10% | Polls, replies |

**Hashtags:**
- Primary: #Shopify #Ecommerce #CRO #A/BTesting
- Secondary: #Startup #SaaS #SmallBusiness #Growth

## Engagement Strategy

### Responding to Comments

| Type | Response Time | Style |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Questions | Within 1 hour | Helpful, detailed |
| Feedback | Within 2 hours | Thankful, actionable |
| Complaints | Within 30 min | Apologize, solve |
| Spam | Ignore/delete | Professional |

### Engaging with Others

| Action | Daily Goal |
|--------|------------|
| Like relevant posts | 10 |
| Comment on others | 5 |
| Retweet有价值 content | 5 |
| Follow relevant accounts | 10 |
| Reply to mentions | All |

### Hashtag Usage

**Optimal per post:**
- 2-3 primary hashtags
- 2-3 secondary hashtags
- 1 app-specific hashtag

**Never use:**
- #followforfollow
- #likeforlike
- Generic spam hashtags

## Crisis Management

### If Negative Reviews

1. Acknowledge quickly
2. Apologize sincerely
3. Take offline
4. Solve problem
5. Follow up publicly

### If Technical Issues

1. Be transparent
2. Update frequently
3. Compensate affected users
4. Share fix timeline
5. Thank for patience
