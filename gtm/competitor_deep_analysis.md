## WHAT WE FOUND

### Convert (convert.com) - Current Positioning

**Tagline:** "Reliable & Affordable. 40,000 sites optimized over 15 years"

**Key Messages:**
- "Improved Decision Making, Innovation & Growth"
- "Confidently run tests across all your growth channels"
- "Affordable full-stack tool"
- "4x faster support"

**Features Claimed:**
- Server-side implementation (cookie issues)
- Feature flags and rollouts
- 40+ targeting filters
- Headless version available
- Frequentist and Bayesian stats engines
- SRM checks, collision prevention
- Flicker-free
- 1st party cookies only
- GDPR & ePrivacy compliant

**Pricing Model:**
- Premium positioned
- "Full stack" = enterprise focus
- 15 years in market = trust/authority

**Marketing Approach:**
- Technical sophistication
- "15 years experience"
- Support speed as differentiator
- Enterprise features

---

**Tagline:** "World's leading AI-powered digital experiences"

**Key Messages:**
- "AI-powered"
- Digital experience platform (not just A/B testing)
- Enterprise scale
- Full stack experimentation

**Positioning:**
- Market leader
- Enterprise only
- AI-first messaging
- Very expensive ($500+/mo)

**Marketing Approach:**
- AI narrative
- Enterprise sales

- Company size focus

## WHAT WE LEARNED

### Their Strengths:

1. **Convert:** Long track record, technical depth, trust
2. **Optimizely:** AI narrative, enterprise features, market leader

### Their Weaknesses:

1. **Convert:** Expensive for SMB, complex for beginners
2. **Optimizely:** Way too expensive for most Shopify merchants ($500+/mo)
3. **All:** No true "free for SMB" options

### Our Positioning Opportunity:

| We Have | They Lack |
|--------|----------|
| Free tier | ❌ None have good free tier |
| SMB focus | ❌ Enterprise only |
| Shopify-native | ❌ Generic tools |
| Simple UI | ❌ Complex/overwhelming |
| Affordable | ❌ Expensive |
