### How Cartboost Works

Testing with Cartboost is simple:

**Step 1: Create a Test**
Go to the Cartboost app in your Shopify admin. Click "Create a test." Choose what you want to test:
- Product prices
- Product titles or descriptions
- Product images
- Product page layouts
- Homepage designs
- Collection page layouts

**Step 2: Set Your Variants**
Your original version (Control) goes against your new idea (Variant). For a price test, it might be $19 vs. $29. For copy, it might be "Limited edition" vs. "Bestseller." For images, it's which photo converts better.

**Step 3: Launch & Let It Run**
Cartboost automatically splits your traffic. Half your visitors see the Control. Half see the Variant. We track impressions, clicks, and conversions for both.

**Step 4: Get Results**
Within 1-2 weeks (depending on traffic), you'll have statistical significance. You'll know which version actually drives more revenue. No more guessing.
